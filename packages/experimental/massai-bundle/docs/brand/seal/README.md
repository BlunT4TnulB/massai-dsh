# The seal — shield, flower crest, M

The owner's "omnisex stamping" question, answered: a **face** carries gender — almond eyes and a slim
jaw read feminine, hard facets read masculine — so a stack of face + flower + M will never be neutral.
Take the face out and the same idea becomes a **seal**: a shield, the Flower of Life as the crest, and
the M filling the field.

## Why this is the neutral one

- A **shield** is a container, not a body — no gender.
- The **flower** is geometry, not anatomy.
- The **M** is the name, not a face.

The earlier face studies (`../app-face/`) stay for the in-app mark beside the wordmark, where a face
is an asset rather than a problem: it makes the product feel attended by something.

## Sizes

`seal-sizes.png` shows the seal at 64 / 32 / 16px. The 16px version is deliberately **not** the same
drawing — the flower is dropped and the shield and M are fattened, because at that size the crest is
indistinguishable from noise.
