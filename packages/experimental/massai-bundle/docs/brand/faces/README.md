# The face

The owner wanted a face. Every AI render in the reference set gave an armoured fantasy mask —
beautiful and unusable as a logo. These are faces built to *reduce*: a gold helm silhouette, a
dark V opening, two glowing eyes. Nothing else.

## The two files that matter

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `n1-plain.svg` → `face-plain-1024.png` | the helm: gold shell, V visor, two ember eyes |
| `n2-horned.svg` → `face-horned-1024.png` | the same face with two horns — more deity, less relic |

`refined.png` shows both at 300px and at 16/24/32px (nearest-neighbour, so nothing is flattered).

## The thing worth noticing

**The visor is a V.** The face's opening is the owner's initial — the same V as the forked horns in
the sigil family, now doing the work of a mouth and a visor at the same time. So this is not a third
unrelated idea: it is the sigil's V, worn as a face.

## Why it survives

- Three shapes: shell, opening, two eyes. At 16px that is a gold blob, a dark wedge and two dots —
  still a face, which is more than any armoured render can claim.
- It is gold-on-black, so it works on the door, in the tab, on a phone tile and on a printed patch.
- The eyes are the only warm accent, so the face has one focal point rather than seven.

## Honest limits

- Without horns it can read as a "helmet" or a "shark head" rather than a face.
- With horns it can read as demonic; that may be exactly right, or exactly wrong.
- Two eyes and a V are close to a *smiley* in silhouette; the vertical pupils are what stop it.
