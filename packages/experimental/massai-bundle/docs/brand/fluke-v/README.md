# The fluke-V mark

The whale's tail is the monogram: two flukes meeting at a point make a **V**, and the
gold spout above says "whale" without drawing one. It replaces the pixel-whale crest,
which read as a bitmap at small sizes and as an animal at large ones.

## Why this one

- **Reads at 16px.** A V survives any amount of downscaling; a whale silhouette does not.
- **It is both a mark and a monogram.** No need for a separate "V" anywhere.
- **The source is the animal.** The flukes are drawn from a real fluke outline, not invented
  geometry, and the spout is three drops rather than a stylised fountain.
- **Blue is earned.** The blue is the whale only — rules from `../BRAND.md` still hold.

## What it replaces

`../crest.svg` (pixel whale in a shield) and `../crest-v2.svg` (whale-tail shield with a
beaded halo). Those keep their place in the archive; nothing is deleted, because the fork
is public and someone may prefer the old one.
