# The crest, read symbol by symbol

The chosen master is `massai-crest-chosen.jpg`. `symbols-annotated.png` numbers every
element on the artwork. This file explains what each one is doing, so nobody has to guess
later — and so the flat icon keeps only what matters.

## Above the shield

**1 · The V visor.** The helm's face is a squared **V** and its mouth a second, inverted V.
The V is the owner's initial and the letter this whole mark hangs on. A face that cannot be
seen: the intelligence is behind the door, not in the doorway.

**2 · The crown.** Sovereignty over the tool. The workshop belongs to whoever holds the key —
not to the vendor. Crown over visor says *we rule the machine, it does not rule us.*

**3 · The halo and the sunburst.** "Massai" is one letter from "Messiah". The halo
acknowledges the shadow the name casts, and the sunburst keeps it as **light, not title** —
a claim of honesty, not divinity.

**4 · The mantling.** The black-and-gold acanthus spilling either side is heraldry's oldest
sign of lineage. Here it is the fork itself: *roots and branches*. Fork it, branch it.

## The medallion ring (the "language" of the crest)

Reading clockwise: **spiral** (craft, the nautilus — growth by small turns), **four-point
star** (a guiding star), **sun** (energy), **grille / portcullis** (the gate — this is a
doorway product), **M** (Massai), **gear** (engineering, the work being done), **linked
rings** (continuity, connection), **triangle in circle** (an alembic — transformation),
**diamond lattice** (structure, a chart or a tag), **grid glyph** (the matrix: many small
cells, many small decisions).

No zodiac, no mysticism for its own sake: it is a vocabulary of *disciplines* — astronomy,
engineering, mathematics, craft, the name.

## Inside the shield

**9 · The star.** Guidance at the top centre, where a helm's crest jewel would sit.

**8 · The hash grid.** A 4×4 lattice — computation itself, the matrix of tokens.

**10 · The knot.** A woven diamond of four loops: Celtic/Islamic knotwork, the same family as
the "open by default" lattice. Interconnection, inheritance, things that hold without glue.

**11 · The whale.** DeepSeek's animal, and the engine. Blue appears here and **only** here —
the one colour the whale is allowed to own.

**12 · The harness.** This is the detail worth keeping. The whale is **yoked**: collar,
buckles, rings. The engine is not wild weather; it is a draft animal put to work by the
person holding the reins. The baleen — those parallel plates in its jaw — are the many fine
filters that turn a mouthful of ocean into a meal.

## The supporters

**13 · The lion.** Heraldic courage, king of beasts — and the Maasai rite of the lion hunt.
Strength that serves rather than threatens.

**14 · The warrior.** Spear, shield, red shuka, beadwork. The namesake people: the human
partner standing beside the machine, armed with patience rather than power. Note the balance —
beast on one side, human on the other, shield between them.

**15 · The scroll.** The name, asserted plainly. No tagline needed here.

## Colour

Ember and ochre `#C1440E` for earth, forge and the Maasai shuka; gold `#C9A227` for the halo
and the filigree; deep blue-black `#0B1020`→`#151517` for the ocean the whale lives in; bone
`#F2F2F4` for the visor, the only pure light in the frame.

## What the flat icon keeps

From this artwork, in priority order: **the V** (visor), **the halo** (ring above), **the
whale tail** (blue, below). Everything else — crown, mantling, knotwork, lion, warrior, scroll —
lives in the crest tier and the artwork tier, never in the 16px icon. See `README.md`.

## Verification note — the glyph check

Before this mark was adopted, every glyph in the medallion ring was inspected at full
magnification, because a low-resolution crop of a square glyph can read as a meander or worse.

- `verify-glyphs-left.png` — the two left medallions: a **rounded square with interior hooks
  (grille / portcullis)** and a **diamond lattice** with a small pennant. Neither is a
  swastika, and no rotation of them produces one.
- `verify-glyphs-right.png` — the right side: **M**, a **gear/cog**, and two **linked rings**.
- `verify-first-halo.png` — the earlier draft's halo, which carries **ornamental vine
  filigree only** and no glyphs at all.

Rule going forward: no symbol ships without a full-resolution look, and no such concern is
mentioned next to a design until it has actually been checked.
