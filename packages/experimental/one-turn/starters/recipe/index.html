Garlic, lemon, and too much parsley

For a night when the fridge is nearly empty and that is fine.

20 min totalserves 2one pan

You need

  • 200 g pasta
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced thin
  • rind and juice of half a lemon
  • a big handful of parsley, chopped
  • olive oil, salt, chilli flakes if you have them

Do this

  1. Boil the pasta in well-salted water. Keep a mug of the water before draining.
  2. Warm a good slick of olive oil, add garlic, cook until it smells like the whole kitchen. Do not let it brown.
  3. Add the lemon rind and a pinch of chilli. Stir.
  4. Drain the pasta, tip it in, splash in the pasta water, toss hard until it looks glossy.
  5. Off the heat: lemon juice, parsley, salt. Toss again. Serve immediately.

Notes

If it looks dry, it wants water, not more oil. Cheese is not required but never hurts.
